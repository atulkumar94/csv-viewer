CSV Viewer

Privacy policy · Last updated 14 October 2026

Summary

The files you open with CSV Viewer are processed entirely in your web browser, on your own device. Their contents are never uploaded, stored or seen by us or anyone else.

Files you open

When you choose or drop a file, your browser reads it locally and builds the table on your device. The file and its contents are never sent over the internet. The page's security settings (its Content Security Policy) block it from making network requests, so it could not send your data even by mistake. When you close or reload the page, the data is discarded.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

CSV Viewer does not use cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising, and it does not load any third-party scripts, fonts or images.

Information collected by our hosting provider

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages may automatically log basic technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. Hosting providers use these logs for security and to keep the service running. These logs never contain the contents of your files.

Children

CSV Viewer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if advertising is added to the site, this page will be updated before the change takes effect, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

For questions about this policy, feedback or bug reports, open an issue or start a discussion on GitHub. Issues and discussions are public, so please don't include personal information or the contents of your files.
